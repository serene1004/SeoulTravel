## Design Context

### Users

First-time visitors planning a day in Seoul, especially Japanese portfolio reviewers who need to quickly understand how a map-centric travel utility solves place discovery, nearby exploration, route planning, and weather preparation.

### Brand Personality

Warm, poised, and encouraging. The interface should preserve the calm confidence of a well-made travel tool, with just enough softness to evoke anticipation rather than a childlike mood.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined Seoul travel notebook: warm cream paper, muted coral for location and a quiet sage route accent. Rounded details and gentle travel cues create warmth, but illustrations, color, and motion remain restrained. The home is a welcoming launchpad, while map exploration, spot details, and routes have their own focused pages.

### Design Principles

- Lead with an inviting next adventure, then make the map and next decision easy to find.
- Make location, travel time, and route state visible at a glance.
- Use curated information with subtle, purposeful warmth rather than generic card grids.
- Translate raw data into helpful travel guidance, including weather and clothing.
- Keep every core interaction accessible, responsive, and easy to reverse.
